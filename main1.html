Kerala local body election results 2025: UDF bags 619 seats, LDF 566 and BJP 69

election result

Kerala local body election results 2025: The UDF has won 619 seats, the LDF 566 and the BJP 69 in the local body elections whose counting was held on Saturday (December 13, 2025). The elections were held in two phases on December 9 and 11.
